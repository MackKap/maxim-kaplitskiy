↗ Website ↗ Client feedback

B2B ESG SaaS for the German market — helping mid-sized companies meet CSRD/ESRS compliance requirements. Built the entire platform from scratch, progressed from sole engineer to leading a cross-functional team of 13+ people.

  • Platform scope — four full modules built and maintained: Sustainability Reporting, Carbon Accounting (Scope 1/2/3), Supplier Audit Management, ESG Project Management.
  • AI reporting pipeline — automated ESRS report generation using company onboarding documents. Cut preparation time from 3,000+ to under 900 staff-hours. One of the first ESG platforms in Europe to take this approach, built in 2023.
  • Report generation pipeline — integrated Docmosis end-to-end: reporting data → processing → final formatted document ready for submission.
  • Airtable integration — connected Airtable as a data entry interface for ESG framework experts, eliminating the need to work directly inside the platform.
  • Custom TinyMCE plugin — co-developed with a Python engineer from scratch; no existing plugin had the required functionality, so we built one.
  • Multi-tenant architecture — Supabase RLS with two-level access control by company role and individual permissions. GDPR-compliant data residency.
  • Enterprise SSO — full Microsoft Entra ID (Azure AD) integration built from scratch after hitting tooling limitations with a custom OAuth flow.
  • Team & delivery — hired and led up to 5 direct reports; sprint planning, stakeholder management, feedback-to-release in as little as 1 hour, critical incident response under 10 minutes.
  • Delivery process design — eliminated direct ad-hoc client contact with developers by designing structured communication protocols, protecting team focus and reducing context switching across active sprints.
  • People management — retained a key engineer at risk of leaving by restructuring role conditions and incorporating personal development support into their engagement.
  • Investor readiness — delivered production-ready product that contributed to the company's €430K seed round.
